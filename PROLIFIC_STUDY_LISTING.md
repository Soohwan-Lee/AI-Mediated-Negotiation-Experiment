# Prolific Study Listing

## Study Name

Workplace Negotiation and AI-Mediated Communication

## Study Description

We are inviting adults aged 18 or older who can read and write English comfortably and have at least one year of work experience. This study takes about 45 minutes and must be completed on a desktop or laptop.

You will take an assigned role in two fictional workplace negotiation tasks. In one task, you will chat directly with another participant. In the other, an AI Proxy will speak for you first, followed by a direct conversation about the proposed agreement. The study also includes brief background questions, short tutorials and practice rounds, and questions about your experience after each task.

All scenarios and role information are fictional. Please do not share real private or identifying information. Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time without penalty. You will receive the payment shown on Prolific; the study will explain any additional role-based payment, if applicable.
